# FAQ

**What is DigitMoney OS?**  
An advanced personal financial operating system that manages, grows, and protects on-chain capital through natural language.

**What is $DIGIT?**  
The native token of DigitMoney OS, launched on Base via Bankr. It powers the economic layer and aligns the community with the success of the agent.

**Is the supply fixed?**  
Yes. 100 billion tokens, non-mintable.

**Who gets the trading fees?**  
The DigitMoney OS agent/treasury receives 0.665% of all trading volume. These fees fund compute, development, and future community incentives.

**Is there a team allocation?**  
Yes — 15% of supply vests linearly over 1 year with a 30-day cliff. This is fully transparent via Bankr’s standard mechanism.

**Will there be an airdrop?**  
Any future community distributions will be clearly announced and executed transparently. There is no hidden allocation at launch.
