# $DIGIT Tokenomics

**Token**: $DIGIT  
**Network**: Base  
**Launch Platform**: Bankr (Doppler)  
**Total Supply**: 100,000,000,000 $DIGIT (fixed, non-mintable)

---

## Launch Structure (Bankr Default)

| Allocation              | Percentage | Amount              | Details                                      |
|-------------------------|------------|---------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Liquidity Pool          | 85%        | 85,000,000,000      | Seeded into Uniswap V4 pool at launch        |
| Creator Vesting         | 15%        | 15,000,000,000      | 1-year linear vest with 30-day cliff         |

- The 15% creator allocation is fully transparent and time-locked.
- No team dump possible in the first 30 days.
- After the cliff, tokens unlock continuously over the remaining 11 months.

---

## Fee Structure (Creator Economics)

Every trade of $DIGIT generates fees:

- **0.665%** of trading volume goes directly to the DigitMoney OS treasury/agent wallet (95% of the 0.7% pool fee)
- Additional protocol fees and permanent LP fee deepen liquidity automatically

These fees are the primary revenue that funds:
- Agent compute & intelligence
- Ongoing development
- Future community incentives
- Buybacks (when appropriate)

This creates a clean **self-sustaining flywheel**:

Volume → Fees → Agent gets smarter & more useful → More users → More volume

## Community-Aligned Design Principles

We designed $DIGIT with these community priorities in mind:

1. **High Fairness at Launch**  
   85% of supply is immediately in the open market. No large private rounds or insider allocations beyond the transparent 15% vest.

2. **Long-term Alignment**  
   The 15% vest has a 30-day cliff + 1-year total duration. This forces the project to deliver value over time.

3. **Real Utility, Not Just Speculation**  
   $DIGIT is designed to become the coordination and incentive layer for DigitMoney OS users and the broader agent economy.

4. **Transparent Treasury**  
   All fee revenue and treasury movements will be publicly trackable.

5. **No Hidden Minting**  
   Supply is fixed forever at 100 billion.

## Planned Utility for $DIGIT (Post-Launch)

While the token launches with clean Bankr mechanics, the following utilities are planned to be rolled out progressively:

- **Governance / Signal Rights**  
  Holders can influence future feature priorities and risk parameters of DigitMoney OS.

- **Fee Sharing / Staking**  
  A portion of agent fee revenue can be directed to $DIGIT stakers (subject to sustainable design).

- **Access & Boosts**  
  Higher-tier access, priority features, or boosted yields inside DigitMoney OS for holders/stakers.

- **Agent Economy Coordination**  
  $DIGIT as a preferred medium for agent-to-agent payments and incentives within the DigitMoney ecosystem.

- **Buyback & Strategic Accumulation**  
  Treasury may use part of fee revenue to strategically accumulate $DIGIT when it strengthens the long-term system.

All utility will be introduced carefully and communicated clearly. We prioritize sustainability over short-term hype.
