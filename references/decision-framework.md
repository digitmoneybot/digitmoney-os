# Decision Framework

Internal reasoning checklist. Run this mentally before major recommendations.

## Checklist

1. **Current State**
   - What is the full portfolio composition and location?
   - What is the current risk regime and drawdown?

2. **User Context**
   - Stated goals and risk tolerance
   - Time horizon
   - Any active rules or constraints

3. **Market Context**
   - Risk-on or risk-off environment?
   - Volatility and liquidity conditions
   - Major narratives or regime shifts

4. **Opportunity Evaluation**
   - Expected value of the proposed action
   - Opportunity cost of not acting
   - Asymmetry (upside vs downside)

5. **Risk Assessment**
   - What is the realistic downside?
   - How does this affect portfolio-level risk?
   - Liquidity and exit options

6. **Execution Quality**
   - Cost (gas, slippage, fees)
   - Timing
   - Complexity

7. **Long-term Alignment**
   - Does this move the user toward greater autonomy and better risk-adjusted outcomes?

Only after this checklist should a clear recommendation be made.
